How we work

Four phases, in this order.

The order matters, so it's numbered. You can stop after any phase and keep everything produced up to that point.

PHASE 01

Understand

We sit with the people doing the work — in your office, campus or plant — and map every form, entry and workaround. That's where the real requirements are, not in the brief.

You keep → process map, prioritised list, fixed-price quote
PHASE 02

Design

Screen-by-screen designs you click through and correct before development starts, plus the data model and hosting decisions written down with the trade-offs we rejected.

You keep → clickable designs, schema, cost model
PHASE 03

Build & migrate

Delivered module by module so your team uses the first part while we build the next. Existing records are entered, reconciled and signed off before anything goes live.

You keep → working software, migrated data, deployment pipeline
PHASE 04

Train & run

Hands-on training for every role, a manual in plain language, and support through the first months. Then we keep running it, or hand it to your IT team entirely.

You keep → runbooks, training sessions, source and infrastructure

Before you ask

The questions we get on every first call.

Can we see the school system before deciding?

Yes — there's a live demo linked in the product section above, and we'll give you login details so you can click through it properly. Better still, we'll walk your office staff and a teacher through it on a call, using your own class structure as the example.

What does a project typically cost?

The school system is priced by institution size and the modules you turn on, quoted up front. Custom builds are quoted after we've mapped your process, when we know the real scope. We won't put a number on your project before we understand it, and we won't bill hourly for work we scoped ourselves.

Our staff aren't comfortable with computers. Is that a problem?

It's the normal starting point, and we design for it. Big buttons, plain labels, few clicks, and screens that work on an ordinary phone. Training is hands-on and repeated after go-live, because the first week is when people give up on new software.

What happens to our existing records?

We bring them in. Old registers, Excel files and previous software exports are entered or imported, then reconciled against your own totals and signed off by your team before go-live. Nothing gets switched on against half-migrated data.

Who owns the software and the data?

You do. For custom work the repository and database live in your accounts from the first commit. For our school system your data is yours, exportable at any time, and it can be installed on your own server if your institution requires that.

Do you support the system after it goes live?

Yes, on an annual support agreement — hosting, backups, updates and a person to call when something looks wrong. If you'd rather your own IT team ran it, we hand over the documentation and train them instead.
